## Material earlier-use findings

Existing game/public uses directly relevant to the current naming family include:

1. `Light in the dark` — existing videogame title already used commercially.
2. `A Light in the Dark` — CreSpirit / STORIA / Narrator, published by Sekai Project — Steam release 2018-06-15.
3. `Les Veilleurs` is already used for a published solo gamebook and is also a recurrent title/expression in books and other media.
4. `Watcher Chronicles` uses “Les Veilleurs” in its French game description for a dark-fantasy group, making the expression less distinctive in our genre context.
5. The exact expression `Veilleurs des Cendres` is already publicly used in another French fantasy/lore universe online. Separately, Dark Souls III prominently combines the nearby genre vocabulary `Veilleurs des Abysses` and `Seigneurs des Cendres`. These are not, by themselves, proof of trademark conflict, but they reduce the practical distinctiveness of `Les Veilleurs des Cendres` for a dark-fantasy game.
6. `Hope from Ashes` is already used exactly as the name of a faction quest in **Crimson Desert** (2026), a current fantasy videogame. The exact expression is also present in music as an artist/title. This does not automatically bar trademark registration, but it materially weakens distinctiveness and creates store/search confusion risk in the same entertainment field.
7. `The Concord of Ashes` is already the title of a 2026 fantasy/sci-fi audiobook/novel, so `Concord of Ashes` is not preferred as an English commercial title.
8. `The Ashen Concord` is already used as the title of a 2026 D&D campaign/world project, reducing distinctiveness.
9. `The Cinder Concord` is already used as the name of a fantasy organization dedicated to reconstruction in a public World Anvil setting, very close thematically to LITD.
10. `Concord of Cinders` is already used as a political institution/treaty in an active fantasy web novel.
11. `Beyond the Ashes`, `From Ashes We Rise`, `Embers of Tomorrow`, `Ashen Rebirth`, and `Ashes into Dawn` all have existing game/mod/music/media uses and are not preferred.

These findings do not by themselves prove infringement or make registration impossible. Earlier rights, territories, similarity of signs, goods/services and actual registrations must be examined.

## English naming shortlist — current recommendation

### 1. THE LAST EMBER ACCORD

Current best English candidate from the public-web elimination pass.

Why it fits LITD:

- `Last Ember` carries hope, survival and fragility without using the highly crowded formula `from ashes`;
- `Accord` expresses politics, cooperation and reconstruction, one of LITD's distinguishing pillars;
- the phrase sounds like both a world-defining pact and a game title;
- shorter and more natural in English than `Concord of the Last Ember`;
- does not depend on the uncleared `LITD` acronym.

Status: **YELLOW-GREEN PRELIMINARY — formal trademark clearance still mandatory.**

### 2. CONCORD OF THE LAST EMBER

More ceremonial and lore-driven. It preserves the direct concept of `Concorde` and links it to a surviving ember. Strong for an RPG universe, but less immediate in store listings.

Status: **YELLOW-GREEN PRELIMINARY.**

### 3. HOPE BEYOND ASHES

Closest emotional successor to `Hope from Ashes`. It keeps the hope/rebirth meaning while avoiding the exact `Crimson Desert` quest phrase. However, because it is a relatively descriptive poetic phrase, it may be less distinctive as a trademark than the two `Accord/Concord` candidates.

Status: **YELLOW-GREEN PRELIMINARY, lower distinctiveness confidence.**

## Recommended naming path

1. Keep `LITD : Les Veilleurs` as internal/dev wording only.
2. Put **LA CONCORDE DES CENDRES** at the top of the French professional search list.
3. Put **THE LAST EMBER ACCORD** at the top of the English professional search list.
4. Keep **CONCORD OF THE LAST EMBER** as the English alternate.
5. Keep **HOPE BEYOND ASHES** as the emotional/poetic alternate, but do not assume it will be as protectable.
6. Keep **HOPE FROM ASHES** as a thematic phrase, quest/chapter/tagline candidate only if later review allows it; do not lock it as the primary game title.
7. Demote **LES VEILLEURS DES CENDRES** from the preferred shortlist.
8. Do not spend on final logos/store capsules using `Light in the Dark` until the professional search is complete.
9. If the universe needs a master brand, search `LITD` independently rather than assuming the acronym is safe because the long form changes.
10. Until title clearance, create a **text-free brand emblem** and keep the final wordmark blocked.

## Formal search scope required before branding lock

- France: INPI / DATA INPI + similarity search.
- European Union: EUIPO / TMview.
- International registrations relevant to France/EU: WIPO/Madrid data via appropriate databases.
- Company/trade names and domain names relevant to the selected title.
- Unregistered earlier uses where legally relevant.

A public-web query on 2026-09-08 did not surface an obvious exact result for `THE LAST EMBER ACCORD` or `CONCORD OF THE LAST EMBER` on ordinary public-web search. That absence is **not** a database clearance and must not be treated as evidence that either mark is legally available.

## Goods/services

At minimum review Nice Class 9 for downloadable game software and Class 41 for entertainment/game services if offered. Add other classes only when the actual business model/merchandising/services warrant them; do not file a broad class list mechanically.

## Decision rule

**NO-GO:** do not file, print packaging, buy a large branding campaign, or present any candidate as legally secured until a French/EU trademark professional (CPI or qualified lawyer) has reviewed the exact sign, territories and goods/services.
