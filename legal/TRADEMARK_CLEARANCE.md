## Material earlier-use findings

Two existing commercial videogame titles are directly relevant to the verbal sign:

1. `Light in the dark` — D1ONE — Steam release 2018-01-09.
2. `A Light in the Dark` — CreSpirit / STORIA / Narrator, published by Sekai Project — Steam release 2018-06-15.

Because these are videogames, they are much more relevant than a same/similar expression used for unrelated goods. Their existence does not by itself prove trademark infringement or make registration impossible; earlier rights, territories, similarity of signs, goods/services and actual registrations must be examined.

## Search scope required before branding lock

- France: INPI / DATA INPI + similarity search.
- European Union: EUIPO / TMview.
- International registrations relevant to France/EU: WIPO/Madrid data via appropriate databases.
- Company/trade names and domain names relevant to the selected title.
- Unregistered earlier uses where legally relevant.

## Goods/services

At minimum review Nice Class 9 for downloadable game software and Class 41 for entertainment/game services if offered. Add other classes only when the actual business model/merchandising/services warrant them; do not file a broad class list mechanically.

## Decision rule

**NO-GO:** do not file, print packaging, buy a large branding campaign, or present `Light in the Dark` as legally secured until a French/EU trademark professional (CPI or qualified lawyer) has reviewed the exact sign, territories and goods/services.

`LITD : Les Veilleurs` may remain an internal/development working designation during this review. A final commercial title can be substituted without changing game-system identifiers where practical.
