# LITD — ENGLISH NAMING SHORTLIST

Date: 2026-09-08
Purpose: severe public-web elimination pass before professional trademark clearance.

This is **not** a trademark clearance or legal opinion. A favorable result means only that the title survived the current public-web/game-store collision scan better than rejected alternatives.

## Severe filter used

A candidate must satisfy all four:

1. strong English sound and memorability;
2. clear thematic fit with LITD: ashes, hope, reconstruction, memory, politics/common good;
3. no obvious exact collision surfaced in the current public-web/game-store pass;
4. better distinctiveness than generic `hope + ashes` wording.

## Rejected/demoted roots from this pass

- `EMBERWAKE`: existing Steam game and other active game projects; reject this root for the commercial title.
- `CINDERWAKE`: active game/app/tabletop uses; reject this root.
- `THE EMBERBOUND ACCORD`: exact public D&D campaign title; reject exact phrase.
- `THE EMBER CONCORDAT`: already publicly used as a team name; demote.
- `THE ASHEN COMPACT`: exact active fantasy-fiction use; reject exact phrase.
- `THE FIRE WE INHERIT`: 2026 published novel; reject exact phrase.
- `THE ASHES WE CARRY`: existing song/chapter/media uses; reject as primary commercial title.
- `THE EMBER ASSEMBLY`: existing music artist/design-agency identity; reject exact phrase.

## Final five — first-pass ranking

### 1. THE LAST EMBER ACCORD

**Status: YELLOW-GREEN PRELIMINARY**

Best current English candidate.

Why it works:

- `Last Ember` = surviving hope after collapse;
- `Accord` = politics, common good, alliances and reconstruction;
- natural English rhythm;
- short enough for store capsules and trailers;
- works with a text-free emblem;
- not dependent on `LITD` or `Light in the Dark`.

Main caution: `ember`, `last ember`, and `accord` are individually common fantasy terms. Formal similarity search remains mandatory.

### 2. THE REKINDLED ACCORD

**Status: YELLOW-GREEN PRELIMINARY**

Why it works:

- directly communicates rebuilding after catastrophe;
- `Rekindled` adds agency: the world is not simply surviving, it is deliberately restarted;
- `Accord` preserves the political/common-good pillar;
- shorter and more distinctive than descriptive `hope from ashes` formulas.

Main caution: `rekindled` is common fantasy vocabulary; similarity review must include `Rekindled`, `Rekindling`, and close `Accord` titles.

### 3. THE LAST HEARTH ACCORD

**Status: YELLOW-GREEN PRELIMINARY**

Why it works:

- `Hearth` shifts the identity from generic fire toward **home, community, civilization and sanctuary**;
- strongly aligned with the Sanctuary and reconstruction loop;
- carries less direct dark-fantasy cliché than `ashes` or `cinders`;
- visually compatible with the emblem/flame language already being developed.

Main caution: slightly less immediately dark than `Last Ember`; marketing would need the visual identity to carry the horror/fall component.

### 4. CINDERBOUND ACCORD

**Status: YELLOW PRELIMINARY**

Why it works:

- coined compound creates better lexical distinctiveness;
- `Cinderbound` implies people bound together by what survived the fall;
- still communicates ash/fire without the exact crowded `from ashes` formula;
- plausible franchise/series title.

Main caution: more synthetic/fantasy-brand sounding than the top three, and `cinder` remains a crowded root. Needs aggressive similarity search before adoption.

### 5. ASHBOUND ACCORD

**Status: YELLOW PRELIMINARY**

Why it works:

- compact, memorable, dark;
- suggests survivors joined by a shared catastrophe;
- directly compatible with LITD's memory/reconstruction themes.

Main caution: `Ashbound` already appears as vocabulary/item naming in fantasy/games even though the exact phrase `Ashbound Accord` did not surface as a major title in this pass. Therefore this is the weakest of the five and should be treated as a reserve candidate.

## Next legal gate

For the top three, perform a professional search covering at minimum:

- INPI / DATA INPI (France);
- EUIPO / TMview (EU);
- WIPO/Madrid records relevant to France/EU;
- Nice Class 9 and Class 41 for the actual game/service scope;
- company names, domains and relevant unregistered uses;
- orthographic, phonetic and conceptual similarity, not exact-match search only.

Until that step is complete, the final wordmark stays BLOCKED and only the text-free emblem may be treated as a production candidate.
