# LITD — AUDIO / MUSIC / VOICE RIGHTS AUDIT

Audit date: 2026-09-08
Status: **ORANGE — systems exist, but no release audio chain of title has been cleared.**

## Repository finding

Audio/music/narrative-audio systems and smoke tests exist, but the extension scans used for this legal pass did not identify project `.ogg`, `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.flac` binaries in the current repository snapshot. Therefore this audit does not claim any soundtrack, SFX or voice recording is legally cleared; it records that no such shipped binary was available to clear in this snapshot.

## Mandatory record for every future audio asset

Assign `AUDIO-LITD-XXXX` and record:

- file/path and final master hash;
- composer/songwriter and composition rights;
- master owner;
- performers and written performer authorization;
- producer/studio where relevant;
- sample pack/library name, license and proof of purchase;
- source recordings and field-recording permissions where relevant;
- generative-AI/tool declaration and terms applicable at creation time;
- voice cloning/synthetic voice consent where applicable;
- Content ID registration/whitelist handling;
- territory, duration, media and permitted adaptations;
- contract/invoice/evidence references.

## Hard gates

- No undocumented sample or ripped game/film/music audio.
- No imitation/cloning of an identifiable real person's voice without the necessary authorization.
- No track marked GREEN solely because a marketplace calls it `royalty free`; inspect the actual license.
- No final release while composition, master and performer rights are ambiguous.
