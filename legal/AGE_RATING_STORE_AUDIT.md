# LITD — AGE RATING / STORE CONTENT AUDIT

Audit date: 2026-09-08
Status: **ORANGE — final rating depends on final presentation and questionnaire answers.**

## Content requiring explicit review

LITD intentionally contains or plans systems concerning anatomy, persistent wounds, bodily consequences and dismemberment. The release audit must therefore treat violence, blood/gore, injury and death as material rating topics rather than generic fantasy combat.

## Do not pre-assign a rating

This document does not assert a PEGI, ESRB, IARC, Apple or Google rating. Authorities/store systems rate the actual content and questionnaire answers; intensity, realism, frequency, camera treatment, gore detail, player agency and other final implementation details matter.

## Final release checklist

For the exact release candidate:

- capture representative worst-case combat/gore footage and screenshots for internal evidence;
- document frequency and realism of blood, dismemberment, exposed anatomy, death and corpse persistence;
- document violence against human/human-like and fantasy characters;
- document fear/horror themes and disturbing imagery;
- document language, sexual content/nudity, drugs/alcohol/tobacco if any;
- document gambling/simulated gambling and paid randomized items if ever introduced;
- complete the applicable Steam content survey;
- complete Google Play/IARC content-rating declarations;
- complete App Store age-rating questionnaire;
- complete any PEGI/ESRB process required by the distribution route/territory;
- ensure store screenshots, trailers and descriptions are consistent with the declared content.

## LITD-specific rule

Any material increase in gore realism, frequency, lingering anatomical detail, torture/sadism framing or player-directed mutilation after rating submission automatically reopens the rating/store audit.
